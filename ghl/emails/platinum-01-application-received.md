## Body

Got it. Your Platinum application is in our hands.

**What happens next:**

1. One of us — Victor or Hako — reads through your application carefully. We're looking at where you are in your case, what kind of support you're looking for, and whether the Platinum tier is the right shape for what you need.
2. We reach out within **one business day** (often sooner) to schedule a follow-up conversation.
3. If you haven't already had a free 15-minute Strategy Call with us, that's where we'll start — it's the cleanest way for both of us to confirm we're a fit before signing on for a recurring partnership.
4. From there: if Platinum fits, we onboard. If something else fits better, we say so.

**A note on capacity.** We keep the Platinum roster intentionally small so the partnership is real, not symbolic. If we're at capacity, we'll be honest with you and offer a short waitlist or suggest Gold as an interim.

**A note on what Platinum is and isn't.** Platinum is ongoing coaching and preparation support — systems, frameworks, document review, prep for the hard meetings. It is **not** legal representation. We'll never draft motions for your case, advise on legal strategy in your specific matter, or replace your attorney.

We'll be in touch.

— Victor & Hako
