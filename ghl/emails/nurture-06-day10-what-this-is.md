## Body

Quick note about what The Primary Trail is, and what it isn't. We say it openly because we'd rather lose a customer to clarity than win one to confusion.

**We are not attorneys.** We don't give legal advice. We don't draft motions. We don't tell you what to do in your specific case. If you don't have a lawyer yet and you have an active case, getting one is step one, and we'll help you think about how to find a good fit.

**We are coaches and educators.** We sell the systems and frameworks we used — and that work with the dads we work with — to walk into the family court system organized, communicate cleanly, document what matters, and make the hours you pay your attorney count.

**Everything we do orients around your kids.** Not against your ex. Not for "dad's rights." Around the children, and around becoming the steadiest, most credible version of yourself in front of the people who decide what's best for them.

That's the line. We hold it.

— Victor & Hako
