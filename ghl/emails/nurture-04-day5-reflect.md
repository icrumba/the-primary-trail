## Preview text
The one practice that prevents most self-inflicted damage.

## Body

If Capture and Organize are about the record, this one is about you.

In a contested case, the most damaging evidence often isn't what the other side produces. It's what you wrote and sent in the worst 60 seconds of a hard day. A text in the parking lot. An email at midnight. A voicemail in a parked car.

Once it's sent, it's part of the record. And it tends to show up at the worst possible time.

The third practice is **Reflect**. It's the discipline of putting 60 seconds — or 6 hours, or 24 hours — between the thing you want to say and the thing you actually send.

It's not weakness. It's the opposite. The dad who can sit with a hot impulse and respond in writing the next morning is the dad whose communications hold up well later.

Some of this is mindset. Some of it is a written process. Some of it is, frankly, asking a calmer human (or, increasingly, an AI tool) to read your draft before you hit send.

All of it is learnable.

— Victor & Hako
