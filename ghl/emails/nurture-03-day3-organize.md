## Preview text
The single most expensive thing you can hand your attorney is a pile.

## Body

Family-law attorneys typically bill $300 to $500 an hour. Some more.

Here's something most dads don't realize until the second invoice: a large share of what they're paying for isn't legal strategy. It's the lawyer or a paralegal *organizing what you brought them*. Sorting your texts. Building a timeline. Putting your emails in date order. Tagging your evidence.

That work has to happen. It just doesn't have to happen on the meter.

The dads who pay the least, get the most out of their attorney, and walk into hearings with the tightest case are the ones who bring their lawyer a packet, not a pile. A timeline, not a folder dump. A labeled set of evidence, not a hard drive of screenshots.

That's the second practice: **Organize**. Take the raw material you captured and turn it into something a professional can use. The framework is simple. The discipline is the part most dads skip.

We help dads build the framework, and then bring it.

— Victor & Hako
