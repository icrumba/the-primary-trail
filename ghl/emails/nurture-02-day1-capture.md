## Body

The first practice we want to put in your hands has nothing to do with court.

It's just this: get everything that happens — every email, every text, every voicemail, every photo, every weird thing said at pickup — into one place. Time-stamped. Searchable. Yours.

That's the practice.

It sounds small. It is the single biggest thing that separates dads who walk into court prepared from dads who walk in scrambling.

Why it matters so much: most dads don't realize until six months in that the documentation they needed was in front of them the whole time — texts they swiped past, screenshots they didn't take, dates they didn't write down. The case turns on the record. And the record is built one quiet capture at a time.

You don't need a system today. You need a place. A folder on your phone. A Google Drive. A notebook by your bed. Anywhere that's yours, that you can find again, that you can hand to your lawyer in one piece six months from now.

We call this **Capture**. It's the first of four practices we'll walk you through this week and next.

— Victor & Hako
