## Body

Got it. Your Diamond application is in our hands.

**What happens next:**

1. One of us — Victor or Hako — reads through your application carefully. We're looking at where you are, what kind of support you need, and whether the in-person element of Diamond will actually work for your geography.
2. We reach out within **one business day** to schedule a follow-up conversation.
3. If you haven't already had a free 15-minute Strategy Call with us, that's where we'll start.
4. From there: if Diamond fits, we onboard. If Platinum or Gold makes more sense for your situation right now, we'll say so. We'd rather right-size the support than oversell it.

**A note on the in-person element.** Diamond includes one in-person session per month and optional attendance at select in-person meetings (mediation, facilitation, evaluations — where permitted). That part of the service is currently Dallas / Collin County, Texas only. If you're outside that area, we may suggest Platinum instead, which keeps everything virtual.

**A note on preparation for facilitations and evaluations.** Our coaching is about helping you show up as the calm, credible, prepared version of yourself — authentically. We don't coach scripted answers. Courts and evaluators see through that, and it works against you. Mock sessions are practice, not performance.

**A note on capacity.** Diamond is the most time-intensive tier on our side, so the roster is the smallest. If we're at capacity, we'll be honest about timing.

**A note on what Diamond is and isn't.** Diamond is high-touch coaching, preparation, and coordination support. It is **not** legal representation. We don't draft motions, advise on legal strategy in your specific matter, or replace your attorney. Our coordination with your attorney (if you want it) is operational, not legal.

We'll be in touch.

— Victor & Hako
