## Body

So you've captured. You've organized. You've slowed down enough to reflect before you respond.

Now: **Execute**.

Execute is the practice of packaging what you've built into something your lawyer can actually use — and, where it's appropriate and allowed, into things that move on their own.

Concretely: a one-page case summary at the front of every folder. A labeled timeline with dates that match the document IDs. A short list of *what you want from this meeting* before every meeting with your attorney. A pre-filled communication log when a Parent Facilitator or evaluator asks for one.

This is where the savings show up. Not because you're doing your lawyer's job — because you're bringing your lawyer the kind of preparation they're used to charging to assemble.

It's also where credibility shows up — in front of the court, in front of evaluators, in front of mediators. A dad who walks in with a packet looks different from a dad who walks in with a pile.

— Victor & Hako
