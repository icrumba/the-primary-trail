QUESTIONS

What dads ask.

Are you lawyers?

No. The Primary Trail is a coaching and education service for dads new to family court. We teach dads how to capture, organize, reflect on, and execute their case. We never draft legal documents, advise on specific legal strategy, or appear in court. Any legal decision should be reviewed with a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction.

How is The Primary Trail different from a general divorce coach?

We focus specifically on dads new to family court and on a four-pillar framework: Capture, Organize, Reflect, Execute. The work is built around what family court actually evaluates: the best interest of the children. The prep helps a dad show up as a credible, organized advocate for his kids, not as someone fighting his ex.

What does the Primary Trail course cover?

Modules map to the four pillars. Capture: how to collect and timestamp every email, text, voicemail, and document without compromising your case. Organize: building a timeline and evidence categories your lawyer can use. Reflect: tone and judgment work, including AI-assisted message review before you hit send. Execute: packaging your case for your lawyer or, where allowed, for direct filing, saving billable hours.

Where is The Primary Trail based and can you help dads outside Texas?

The course and community are open to dads anywhere in the United States. Education and coaching are not state-specific. The 1:1 coaching tier launches in Texas first, with in-person availability in Dallas, Collin, and Denton counties. Additional states will be added as the 1:1 program expands.

Three tiers of 1:1 support are offered. Gold is $175 per hour for ad-hoc consultation. Platinum is $700 per month for an ongoing weekly partnership. Diamond is $1,200 per month for in-person plus virtual full-service support in Dallas and Collin County. Every engagement starts with a free 15-minute strategy call.
