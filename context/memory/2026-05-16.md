# 2026-05-16 — Session log

## Session 1 — Hero copy direction change

### Goal
Make the hero on `public/index.html` bolder and easier. Victor opened with a draft headline along the lines of *"In Texas family court, playing defense is a losing game. The best outcome is earned by taking bold, strategic action — because in this arena the father who leads the charge writes the story."* Asked for a brand check + options.

### Decisions
- **Hero direction:** swap the current headline *"A clear path through family court, built for dads who want to do this right."* for a **Vince Lombardi quote** in the H1 slot, paired with a re-anchored subhead. This supersedes the open "tagline approval" thread from 2026-04-26 (SOUL.md #4 vs. proposed #5) for the hero treatment specifically.
- **Lombardi quote recommended:** *"The will to win is not nearly so important as the will to prepare to win."* — accurately attributed, maps cleanly to the Capture/Organize/Reflect/Execute thesis (preparation > intensity).
- **Avoided:** *"The best defense is a good offense"* — widely misattributed to Lombardi (actually predates him). Putting a misattributed quote on the hero of a brand whose posture is "we don't bullshit you" was rejected on the brand-credibility gate.
- **Subhead chosen:** *"The dad who walks in prepared, organized, and steady shapes the story — the one his kids need told."*
  - Why: keeps the energy of Victor's "defense is a losing game" draft but re-anchors to the children per SOUL non-negotiable #2 (child-best-interest framing) and swaps "leads the charge" (battle vocabulary, on SOUL avoid list) for "walks in prepared" (preferred vocabulary).
- **Texas framing rejected for the hero** (Victor's draft mentioned "Texas family court"). The site/FAQ position the course nationally; pinning the H1 to Texas would narrow the audience without a strategic reason. Texas-first stays scoped to 1:1 coaching only.
- **"Defense is a losing game" line:** parked — likely placement is a section header lower on the page, not a hero element. Decision pending.
